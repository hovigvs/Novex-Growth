"Fill more dates. Recover more inquiries. Never lose track of a lead — or a customer — again."

Most software in this space manages the inquiry you already have. Novex starts one step earlier: it captures every inquiry the moment it arrives, on any channel, in any language — then remembers it, and everyone who's contacted you before, in one place. Nothing about your date, your dietary flags, or your last conversation with a client lives in five different tools that don't talk to each other.

What's real today

  • A voice & chat AI agent that answers instantly, in whatever language the customer uses — no per-language setup
  • Automatic inquiry qualification — event type, date, guest count, dietary flags, budget — before a human ever opens it
  • One shared record per lead and customer, not five disconnected inboxes
  • Tour booking and dietary collection handled automatically, so nothing falls through the cracks before event day
  • Couples/planners design their own room layout on your real floor plan — no emailed sketches back and forth
  • A single dashboard — one login, your business, your data

What's next

Automatic recovery of stalled proposals and past clients gone quiet, and a system that flags exactly which open dates are worth actively marketing — built with real venue and catering input, not guessed at.

We've already built the pattern for a self-serve, AI-assisted ordering website for one of our catering clients — browse the real menu, order by chat or voice with Nicole, multilingual, live today. The venue version of that same idea: prospects browse your actual rooms, packages, and add-ons and configure their own event before ever picking up the phone.
